Civil Law

Understand civil disputes, legal rights, remedies and court procedures in simple language.

What is Civil Law?

Civil law deals mainly with disputes between individuals, organizations or other private parties. It commonly concerns rights, duties, property, contracts, compensation and other non-criminal matters.

Major Areas of Civil Law

Property Disputes

Disputes relating to ownership, possession, transfer and use of property.

Contract Law

Deals with agreements, contractual obligations, breaches and remedies.

Tort Law

Concerns civil wrongs and claims for compensation or other remedies.

Recovery of Money

Legal procedures for recovering money owed under applicable law.

Injunctions

Court orders that may require a person to do or stop doing a particular act.

Civil Appeals

Legal procedures for challenging certain decisions of civil courts.

Common Civil Remedies

Compensation

Financial relief may be awarded where legally justified.

Injunction

A court may grant an order concerning a particular act or conduct.

Declaration

A court may formally determine or declare a legal right or status.

Specific Relief

Certain legal remedies may require performance of an obligation.

Important Civil Law Principles

  • Rights and obligations between parties
  • Burden of proof in civil proceedings
  • Evidence and documentation
  • Alternative dispute resolution
  • Court-based remedies
