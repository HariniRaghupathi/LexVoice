Fundamental Rights

An easy-to-understand overview of constitutional rights guaranteed under Part III of the Constitution of India.

Overview

Fundamental Rights are constitutional rights contained in Part III of the Constitution of India. They provide important protections and freedoms and can be enforced through constitutional remedies, subject to the Constitution and applicable law.

Six Broad Categories

Right to Equality

Articles 14–18

Includes equality before law and related constitutional protections.

Right to Freedom

Articles 19–22

Includes important freedoms and protections concerning personal liberty.

Right Against Exploitation

Articles 23–24

Provides constitutional protections against certain forms of exploitation.

Freedom of Religion

Articles 25–28

Provides constitutional protections relating to freedom of religion.

Cultural & Educational Rights

Articles 29–30

Protects specified cultural and educational interests.

Constitutional Remedies

Article 32

Provides a constitutional mechanism for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

Important Articles

Article 14: Equality before law and equal protection of laws.

Article 19: Protects specified freedoms, subject to constitutional restrictions.

Article 21: Protects life and personal liberty according to law.

Article 21A: Provides for the right to education for children within the constitutional framework.

Article 32: Provides a constitutional remedy for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.
