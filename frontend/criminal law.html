Understanding Criminal Law

Basic information about the criminal justice system.

Criminal law deals with conduct that is treated as an offence under applicable law and establishes rules concerning investigation, prosecution, adjudication and punishment.


In India, the criminal-law framework includes substantive criminal law, criminal procedure and rules concerning evidence. The applicable statutory framework should always be checked for the particular matter and date.

General Criminal-Procedure Flow

A simplified representation of stages that may occur in a criminal case.

01

Complaint / Information

Information concerning an alleged offence may reach the appropriate authorities.

02

Investigation

Authorities may investigate the alleged offence according to applicable procedure.

03

Court Proceedings

A matter may proceed before the appropriate court according to law.

04

Judgment

The court determines the matter based on applicable law and evidence.

Important Criminal-Law Concepts

Basic concepts for students and users exploring criminal law.

Presumption of Innocence

In criminal proceedings, an accused person is generally treated as innocent unless guilt is established according to the applicable legal process.

Burden of Proof

Criminal proceedings involve rules concerning who must establish particular facts and the standard applicable to proof.

Evidence

Courts consider evidence according to applicable evidentiary rules when determining disputed facts.

Fair Trial

Criminal procedure incorporates safeguards intended to ensure proceedings are conducted according to law.

Important Legal Safeguards

General areas of protection relevant to criminal proceedings.

Right to Legal Representation

Legal systems provide mechanisms through which accused persons may obtain legal representation, subject to applicable law.

Protection against Arbitrary Action

Constitutional and statutory safeguards regulate the exercise of powers such as arrest and detention.

Right to Fair Procedure

Criminal proceedings are governed by procedural requirements intended to protect due process.

Right to Appeal

Applicable criminal procedure may provide mechanisms for challenging certain decisions before higher courts.
