Women & Law

Learn about legal protections, workplace rights, family-related rights and legal remedies available under applicable Indian laws.

Overview

Indian law contains various constitutional provisions and statutes addressing equality, safety, employment, family matters and protection from unlawful discrimination or violence.

Important Areas

Equality

Constitutional principles provide important protections concerning equality and non-discrimination.

Workplace Safety

Legal protections address sexual harassment and workplace safety.

Domestic Violence

Legal mechanisms are available concerning domestic violence and related protection.

Marriage & Family

Family laws address matters such as marriage, maintenance and other family disputes.

Maternity Rights

Employment law provides protections concerning maternity in applicable circumstances.

Legal Remedies

Courts and appropriate authorities may provide remedies depending on the matter.

Legal Awareness

  • Know your constitutional and statutory rights
  • Keep important documents and evidence safely
  • Seek professional legal assistance when necessary
  • Use appropriate official complaint mechanisms
  • In emergencies, contact appropriate emergency services
