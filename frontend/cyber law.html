Cyber Law

Understand legal issues involving computers, digital communications, online transactions, privacy and cyber offences.

What is Cyber Law?

Cyber law deals with legal issues connected to computers, digital networks, electronic communications and activities conducted through information technology.

Major Cyber Law Areas

Cyber Crime

Legal issues involving unlawful activities carried out using digital systems.

Online Fraud

Legal concerns involving fraudulent digital transactions and deception.

Data Protection

Legal responsibilities concerning collection, handling and protection of data.

Identity Theft

Legal issues arising from unauthorized use of another person's identity information.

Cyber Harassment

Legal concerns relating to abusive or threatening online conduct.

Electronic Evidence

Digital information that may be relevant to legal proceedings.

Cyber Safety Practices

  • Use strong and unique passwords
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available
  • Do not share confidential credentials
  • Verify suspicious links and messages
  • Keep records of fraudulent or threatening communications
  • Report suspected cyber incidents through appropriate channels
Important: Cyber-law provisions and reporting procedures can change. Always verify the current applicable law and official reporting mechanism before taking legal action.
