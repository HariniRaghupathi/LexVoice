Consumer Law

Learn about consumer rights, defective goods, poor services, complaints and consumer dispute resolution.

What is Consumer Law?

Consumer law provides legal protections for consumers and mechanisms for addressing certain disputes involving goods and services.

Consumer Rights

Right to Safety

Protection against goods and services that may create unreasonable risks.

Right to Information

Consumers should receive relevant information about products and services.

Right to Choose

Consumers may have access to choices among goods and services.

Right to be Heard

Consumer interests may be represented in appropriate forums.

Right to Redressal

Legal mechanisms may be available for addressing consumer grievances.

Consumer Awareness

Consumers can educate themselves about products, services and legal rights.

Common Consumer Complaints

Defective Products

Complaints involving defects in purchased goods.

Poor Services

Disputes involving deficiencies in services.

Unfair Practices

Complaints concerning certain unfair or misleading business practices.

Online Purchases

Consumer issues arising from e-commerce transactions.

Before Filing a Complaint

  • Keep invoices and receipts
  • Save relevant communications
  • Keep photographs or other evidence where appropriate
  • Clearly describe the problem
  • Check the appropriate consumer dispute forum and procedure
