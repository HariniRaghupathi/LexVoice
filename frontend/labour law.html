Labour Law

Understand employment rights, wages, workplace safety, social security and employer-employee relationships.

What is Labour Law?

Labour and employment law regulates various aspects of the relationship between workers and employers, including working conditions, wages, safety and social security, subject to the applicable legal framework.

Major Areas

Minimum Wages

Legal rules concerning minimum wage requirements where applicable.

Working Conditions

Rules and standards relating to workplace conditions and employment.

Workplace Safety

Legal requirements concerning health and safety at work.

Social Security

Employment-related social security and welfare protections under applicable law.

Industrial Relations

Legal framework concerning relations between employers and workers.

Employment Disputes

Mechanisms for addressing certain disputes between workers and employers.

Worker-Related Topics

  • Wages and payment conditions
  • Working hours and leave
  • Workplace health and safety
  • Social security benefits
  • Employment termination and related rights

Employer Responsibilities

Maintain Records

Maintain employment records and documents required by applicable law.

Safe Workplace

Follow applicable workplace health and safety requirements.

Wage Compliance

Legal Compliance

Follow applicable labour and employment regulations.
